Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

SA Opportunity Hub is an independent directory that links to public opportunity providers. At present, the website does not offer user accounts, forms that collect personal details, or direct applications. Its search and category filters run inside your browser.

Information and third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information according to GitHub's policies. When you follow external links, the destination website's privacy practices apply. We do not control their websites.

Cookies and advertising

We have not enabled advertising cookies or a third-party advertising tracker on this website. If advertising or analytics are introduced, this policy and any legally required privacy notices and consent mechanisms will be updated before activation.

Changes

This policy may change when website features change. Review it periodically.

For source corrections, please open an issue in our public GitHub project. Do not include sensitive personal data in public issues.
