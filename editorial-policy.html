Editorial and Source Policy

We provide links to official opportunities for people in South Africa. We are independent and not affiliated with public departments, employers, funders, or tender issuers.

Sources and checks

Listings should link to original publishers wherever possible. A directory or application portal may be included as a navigation resource, and must not be mistaken for a currently open individual opportunity. Application conditions and deadlines must always be checked with the original publisher.

Corrections and expiry

Automated updates can fail or lag and closing dates may change. We aim to remove expired individual listings and correct errors when discovered. You can report a problem on GitHub Issues; avoid posting private information.

Advertising and independence

Advertising may be introduced later. Advertisers will not be permitted to change the meaning of official opportunity information. We do not guarantee employment, funding or tender awards.
